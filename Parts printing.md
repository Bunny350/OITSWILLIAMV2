# Parts printing
This page guides users on printing required parts.

## I want it to live, entertain everyone, be like a boombox and treat it like a computer
The required parts are:
* RealEstate G2
* NUC-specific skirt
* Banger speakers
* 2x 60mm fan mount for 150mm Voron printers
* 1 pair of normal side skirts
* Tracker mounting is generally recommended

## I don't care about making it an enthusatic computer, I've just want it to print, follow as many components as the original, but small
The required parts are:
* SBC-specific skirt
* 2x 60mm fan mount for 150mm Voron printers
* 2 pairs of normal side skirts
* Tracker mounting is generally recommended

## Required spares
This mod requires a dedicated / external power supply, of:
* At least 300W for one with NUC inside,
* Or at least 200W for one with SBC.
