This folder consists of necessary parts for mounting ASUS / Intel NUC 12 Pro to 15 Pro into Voron 2 in 150mm mod.

## Requirements
[View BOM, you may select NUC Mini PC when browsing.](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1hI7gKyXv_dqwRoAh5GxhNzBPyU9dn64bGJ1oHiKoa74/edit?usp=sharing)

### In a nutshell
* Base color of the printer,
* ASUS / Intel NUC 12 Pro, 13 Pro, 14 Pro or 15 Pro, **slim version**
    * Other versions, including the tall version, *Pro Plus*, *Pro AI* or any other variants do not fit. In addition, these printed parts heavily rely on such models of NUC with the metal shell.
